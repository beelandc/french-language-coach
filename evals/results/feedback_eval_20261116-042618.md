## Judge

Judge: `mistral/mistral-large-latest`.

Mistral Large is the default judge (decided in the #231 SPDD analysis). The dataset's conversations, labels and preferred corrections were drafted by a Claude model, so a Claude judge would stack a second Claude-favoring bias on top; Mistral Large shares a provider only with the Mistral baseline. It shares a provider with: mistral-large. Mitigations: the judge only rules on pairs the deterministic pass can't decide, it is not told which model wrote a correction, its task is narrow, and its agreement with hand verdicts is reported by the style of the drafted correction.

## Caveats

- The conversations are synthetic (written to read like typed learner French), not real learner data.
- The conversations, labels and preferred corrections were drafted by a Claude model and reviewed by one maintainer who self-assesses at about B1; Claude models may agree more often with labels written in Claude's style. Advanced-level labels carry the least authority, so read the per-difficulty tables.
- The judge-validation verdicts were drafted by Claude Code for review by the maintainer; see evals/README.md for their review status.
- The sample is small (27 conversations at most, 71 labeled errors), so small differences between models aren't meaningful.
- The app's feedback prompt asks for example corrections, not every error, which caps recall for every model.
- Each model ran once; outputs are non-deterministic, so a rerun can differ.
- Costs are estimates from data/llm_pricing.json at the checked_on dates listed under Prices.
